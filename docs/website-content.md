## 2. Our Story

### It started in someone else's room
Six of us studied Computer Science at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology, Coimbatore.
We weren't roommates. We lived in different rooms, but each of us, for our own reasons, kept ending up
in the same hostel room. Somewhere between the late nights and the endless conversations, that
room became ours, and the six of us became a team.

### Then we got a house
When we moved out of the hostel, we didn't split up. We rented a house together in Coimbatore
and spent the last two years of college under one roof. Those two years gave us the best vibes
and memories we have, and taught us how the six of us work as one.

`[PHOTO: src/assets/photos/group-photo.jpg — the six of us, a night-time selfie from the college days]`
`[Optional: 1–2 specific memories or inside jokes the team is happy to share publicly]`

### Why "HexaFox"
- **Hexa = six.** There are six of us, like the six sides of a hexagon. Hexagons fit together with no gaps, which is how we cover for each other.
- **Fox = the pack.** Our WhatsApp group has always been called *Nanga Nari Kootam* ("we're a pack of foxes" in Tamil). Foxes are clever, curious and adaptable, and that's how we like to solve problems.
- **The mark.** Our logo blends the Tamil letter **ஆ** (from *ஆறு*, "six") with a fox. It carries our roots and our name in one shape.

### Today
College ended, but the group didn't. We've each spent the years since building real skills in
different corners of tech: AI, frontend, data, cloud, design and consulting. HexaFox is where we bring
those six paths back together.

## 3. Services

### Tech Solutions

**Web & Product Development**
Fast, modern websites and web applications, from a company site to a full product. Built with
React and Next.js and designed to grow with you.

**UX/UI Design**
Research, wireframes, prototypes and polished interfaces in Figma. We design around how your users
actually think, before a line of code is written.

**Data & Databases**
Database design, SQL, ETL pipelines and analytics. We make your data clean, fast and ready to answer questions.

**Cloud & Support**
Deployment, monitoring and ongoing support, so your systems stay up and issues get fixed quickly.

**Consulting & Delivery**
Not sure what you need yet? We help you map the problem, scope the solution, plan the work, and
deliver it on time with clear communication.

### AI Solutions

**AI Assistants & Chatbots**
Assistants that answer from *your* documents and data, not from guesswork.

**AI Features in Your Product**
Add summarisation, search, extraction, classification or smart dashboards to an existing app.

**Workflow Automation & AI Agents**
Hand repetitive, multi-step work to AI agents that use your tools, with a human in the loop where it matters.

**Reliable AI**
The model is the easy part. We build what's around it: the APIs, the data, the retrieval, and the
checks that catch the model when it's wrong.

## 4. How We Work

1. **Understand**: We start with your problem, not our stack. Requirements, goals and constraints, written down and agreed.
2. **Design**: Wireframes and prototypes you can click through before we build.
3. **Build**: Frontend, backend, data and AI, built in short cycles so you see progress every week.
4. **Launch**: Tested, deployed and monitored.
5. **Support & Improve**: We stay on to fix, tune and add what's next.
